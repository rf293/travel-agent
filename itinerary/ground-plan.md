## Time math (so we do not overpack)

From Rome Termini:

- **Florence** — ~1h30–1h40 Frecciarossa. Realistic **day trip**.
- **Venice** — ~3h45–4h. A **day trip is possible but long**; overnight is kinder.
- **Tuscany (Siena / Chianti)** — day tour or 1–2 nights.
- **Amalfi (Positano / Amalfi)** — train to Naples + transfer. Poor as a Rome day trip; better **2 nights** or skip.

You cannot do Florence, Venice, Amalfi, **and** Tuscany as full experiences in ~12 Italy nights without rushing. These Amalfi/Capri notes are a **multi-night coast** list, not a Rome day trip.
